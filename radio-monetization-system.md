## AGENT 9 — AFFILIATE REVENUE

### 5 Affiliate Programs to Join

**1. Amazon Associates — Headphones**
- Products: Sony WH-1000XM5, JBL Tune 770NC, Beats Studio Pro
- Commission: 3–4% per sale
- Promo Script:
  > "If you're tuning into the station and your headphones aren't matching the energy — upgrade. These are what I use. Link below."
  > **CTA:** "Upgrade your sound 🎧 [link]"

**2. Sweetwater / Guitar Center Affiliate — Music Gear**
- Products: Audio interfaces, studio monitors, DJ controllers
- Commission: 4–6%
- Promo Script:
  > "You're vibing to the station, now you want to make your own? Here's exactly what you need to start — no fluff, just the gear that works."
  > **CTA:** "Build your setup 🎚️ [link]"

**3. Epidemic Sound / Artlist — Music Licensing**
- Products: Royalty-free music subscriptions for creators
- Commission: $15–$45 per referral
- Promo Script:
  > "If you're making videos and need music that won't get you copyright-struck — this is what most creators actually use."
  > **CTA:** "Get your music license 🎵 [link]"

**4. Spotify / Apple Music — Streaming Subscriptions**
- Products: Premium music subscriptions
- Commission: Varies by affiliate program (Spotify has an unofficial affiliate via influencer deals; Apple has an official program)
- Promo Script:
  > "For when you want to go deeper on an artist you discovered here — link below to grab their full catalog."
  > **CTA:** "Stream their full discography 🎧 [link]"

**5. NordVPN / ExpressVPN — Utility (High Commission)**
- Commission: $30–$65 per sale
- Why it fits: Music streamers are prime VPN audience
- Promo Script:
  > "Protip: stream without restrictions and keep your data private while you vibe. What I use 👇"
  > **CTA:** "Stay protected while you stream 🔒 [link]"

### Affiliate Link Placement Strategy
- 1 product mention per blog post (natural integration, not ad-feel)
- Sidebar widget: "What We're Listening With" — static affiliate links
- Email newsletter: 1 affiliate product per month, framed as a recommendation
- TikTok bio link: Linktree with top 2 affiliate links alongside station

## AGENT 11 — AD REVENUE

### Tier 1: Display Ads (Immediate)
**WordPress.com Ads (WordAds)**
- Enable via WordPress dashboard → Earn → WordAds
- Revenue: $1–$5 RPM (per 1,000 views)
- At 1,000 views/day = $30–$150/month starting revenue
- Upgrade to WordPress Premium ($8/mo) to unlock full WordAds access

**Google AdSense** (if self-hosted or eligible)
- Apply once you have consistent traffic (500+ daily views)
- Place: Above stream player, sidebar, between blog post sections
- Do NOT place mid-content — kills time on site

### Tier 2: Music Sponsorships
**Target: Independent Artists**
- Offer: "Featured Artist Slot" — $25–$100/month
- What they get: Their song in rotation, a shoutout post, a pinned TikTok
- Pitch script:
  > "We stream 24/7 to a growing audience of hip hop and R&B fans. For $50/month, your track is in rotation and you get a dedicated promo post to our audience. DM to book your slot."
- Where to find artists: Instagram #unsignedartist, Twitter/X hip hop communities, SubmitHub

**Target: Music Blogs / Gear Brands**
- Offer: Sponsored blog post or newsletter mention
- Rate: $50–$200 per placement (grow into this as audience scales)

### Tier 3: Paid Artist Promotion
**"Get Your Song on the Station" Package**
- $15 — 1 week in rotation
- $35 — 1 month + 1 TikTok mention
- $75 — 1 month + dedicated blog post + 2 TikTok posts
- Promote this offer in your TikTok bio and via direct outreach to indie artists
