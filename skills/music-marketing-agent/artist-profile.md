### What faceless + save-focused means in practice

Available pillars: lyric videos, visualizers, hands-on-gear studio shots, beat
breakdowns without a face, text-driven storytelling, cinematic B-roll, audio-forward
clips, gear and process, location shots.

Unavailable: talking-head song stories, reaction videos, face-led POV, fan Q&A to
camera, day-in-the-life vlogs.

The tradeoff to manage: faceless content builds parasocial connection more slowly, and
parasocial connection is what drives saves. Compensate with **a strong recurring visual
signature** (consistent color, type treatment, framing) so posts are recognizable in
under a second, and with **text-driven storytelling** that carries the voice a face
would otherwise deliver.

### Open decision - capacity vs. two projects

3-4 posts/week split across two projects is 1.5-2 posts per project per week, which is
below the sustainable minimum for either. See `workflows/daily-run.md` before planning
around this. Resolve one of:

- **Focus one project** for the 90-day window, park the other. Recommended.
- **Alternate in blocks** - one project for 6 weeks, then the other.
- **Raise capacity** to 6-8/week if that is realistic.

Splitting evenly week-to-week is the option that fails, because neither project
accumulates enough signal for the algorithm to learn who to show it to.
