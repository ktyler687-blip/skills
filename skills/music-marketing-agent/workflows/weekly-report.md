# Weekly Report

Runs once a week. Where the daily run is tactical, this one decides what to stop doing.

## Inputs required

Ask for whatever is missing before writing the analysis sections:

- Spotify for Artists: streams, listeners, saves, playlist adds, source of streams
- Per-post social stats: views, watch time / average view duration, shares, saves, comments, follows gained
- YouTube Studio: impressions, CTR, average view duration
- Email: new subscribers, open rate, click rate
- Website: sessions and referral sources

Partial data is workable. Say which sections are running on partial data rather than
filling gaps with estimates. If nothing was supplied, produce only the sections that do
not require numbers (posting schedule, next week's plan) and say clearly which sections
are blocked and why.

## Report structure

### 1. Scoreboard

Table: metric, this week, last week, change, and whether it is moving toward the primary
90-day goal in the profile. Flag anything moved more than 20% in either direction.

Do not celebrate raw view counts. A million views with 40 saves is a worse week than
20,000 views with 900 saves, and the report should say so.

### 2. What worked

The 2-3 best posts. For each: the number that made it stand out, and the *mechanism* -
hook style, subject, format, length, sound, posting time. Then the reusable rule.

The point is a rule that generates next week's content: "close-up hand shots in the
first frame held retention past 3s on all three posts that used them" is a rule.
"People liked the studio video" is not.

### 3. What underperformed

Same treatment, without flinching. Distinguish:

- **Bad hook** - views low, everything else fine. Fixable, reshoot the first 2s.
- **Bad payoff** - views fine, retention collapses mid-video. Cut the middle.
- **Wrong audience** - retention fine, saves and follows near zero. The content is
  entertaining but not making anyone a fan of the music.
- **Wrong CTA** - engagement fine, conversion zero. The ask was unclear or absent.

That distinction determines the fix. Do not lump them together as "didn't perform."

### 4. Opportunities

New sounds, formats, communities, or moments worth acting on in the next 7 days.
Concrete and dated where possible.

### 5. Peer insight

2-3 comparable artists at a similar level, and what they shipped that worked. Observe
publicly visible activity - format, cadence, framing. Do not fabricate their numbers,
and do not copy their content wholesale; extract the structure and rebuild it in this
artist's voice.

### 6. Trend read

Which trends from the week held, which decayed, which are worth a second post. A format
that worked once is usually worth 2-4 more attempts before it is exhausted.

### 7. Content recommendations

5-7 specific pieces for next week, tied to what section 2 proved. Each with pillar,
song, and format.

### 8. Posting schedule

A day-by-day grid for the next 7 days: day, platform, content, time, CTA. Fit it to the
realistic weekly capacity in the profile - do not schedule 21 posts for someone who can
make 4.

### 9. Next week's plan

Three to five actions with owners and days. One should be an experiment: a single
deliberate variable change with a stated hypothesis and the metric that settles it.

Close with: **the one thing to stop doing.** Every report names one. Cutting the weakest
recurring format is usually worth more than adding a new one.
