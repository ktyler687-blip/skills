# Release Campaign

For a single, EP, or album. Adapt the timeline to the actual date in the profile - if the
release is in nine days, compress rather than pretending there are six weeks.

## Phase 0 - Setup (6+ weeks out)

Deadlines are hard here; missing them costs placements that cannot be recovered.

- [ ] Audio mastered and delivered to the distributor
- [ ] **Delivered at least 4 weeks before release date.** This is the gate for editorial
      playlist consideration - a late delivery forfeits it entirely.
- [ ] Metadata locked (see `references/seo-metadata.md`) - exact title casing, features
      credited in the title field, correct genre, writer and producer splits registered
- [ ] Cover art at 3000x3000
- [ ] **Spotify for Artists pitch submitted** - one track, at least 7 days ahead, ideally
      4 weeks. Free, and the single highest-leverage pitch available. Pitching also puts
      the track in Release Radar for followers even if no editorial add happens.
- [ ] Smart link live
- [ ] Canvas (Spotify 3-8s loop) prepared
- [ ] Clips pulled from the session while gear is still set up - studio footage, alt
      takes, hands on instruments, the artist reacting to a playback

## Phase 1 - Tease (4-2 weeks out)

Do not announce the title on day one. Sequence the reveal so there is something new each
week: sound, then context, then title, then date.

- 8-12s snippets of the strongest 15 seconds of the song - the hook, not the intro
- The origin story: what happened that made this song exist
- A visual signature that will repeat across every asset in the campaign
- Presave push, but only alongside a reason to care - a bare presave link converts badly

Target the strongest hook clip at 2 weeks out, when there is still time for it to build.

## Phase 2 - Launch week

**Day -3 to -1:** countdown, best snippet, presave, personal DMs to the artist's most
engaged fans - actual individual messages, not a broadcast.

**Release day:**
- Post within the first two hours of the artist's audience being awake
- CTA is **save**, not stream. Saves and playlist adds signal to Spotify's
  recommendation systems far more strongly than a single play does
- Story with the link sticker, pinned comment with the link, link in bio updated
  everywhere
- Email the list - it is the highest-converting channel the artist owns
- Reply to every comment for the first few hours

**Day +1 to +7:** one post per day minimum. Lyric video, visualizer, reaction to early
listener comments, live take, the song's story in another format. First-week momentum is
what algorithmic playlists read.

## Phase 3 - Sustain (weeks 2-8)

Most artists stop here. That is the mistake - the song is still new to everyone who has
not heard it.

- Keep posting the song for at least 6-8 weeks
- Try 3-5 genuinely different hooks against the same track. A song that flopped on one
  video has not been tested; it has been posted once
- Push user-generated content: ask fans to use the sound, then duet and repost every use
- Second wave: remix, sped-up or slowed version, acoustic, live take, a fan-made edit
- Outreach continues - blogs and podcasts run on weeks-long lead times and many curators
  will not touch a track until it has some traction

## Post-mortem

Two weeks after release, run `workflows/weekly-report.md` scoped to this release:
which asset drove the most saves, which channel drove the most first-time listeners,
what to do differently next time. Append the conclusion to the running log in
`artist-profile.md`.
