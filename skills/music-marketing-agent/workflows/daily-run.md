# Daily Run

The standing daily deliverable. Produces one document the artist can execute without
further thinking. Target working time for them: under 30 minutes of decisions.

Read `artist-profile.md` first. If the primary genre, catalog, or active-platform fields
are unfilled, ask for those before running - the output is worthless without them.

Output all ten sections in order. Keep it tight; this is a work order, not an essay.

---

## 1. Trend scan

Search for current activity in the artist's genre: rising sounds, formats, challenges,
hashtags, and conversation topics.

- With web access: search, then cite what you used. Prefer the last 7-14 days.
- Without web access: say so plainly in one line, then work from durable format patterns
  instead and label them as such.

Report **3-5 items max**, each as: what it is, why it fits this artist specifically, and
the window to act. Discard trends that would require the artist to be someone they are
not - a forced trend entry reads as desperate and converts worse than no post.

## 2. Ten short-form video ideas

Ten concepts for TikTok / Reels / Shorts. Pull structures from
`references/content-engine.md` and spread them across pillars - do not return ten studio
clips.

Each idea is one block:

```
#N - [Working title]
Pillar:      [behind-the-scenes / story / performance / lifestyle / fan Q / trend /
              duet / reaction / lyric / visualizer / cinematic / live]
Song:        [which track this serves]
Hook (0-2s): [exact first frame - what moves, what is said, what is on screen]
Beats:       [3-5 shots with rough timings]
Text on screen: [exact overlay copy]
Audio:       [artist's own track + which section, or named trend sound]
Length:      [seconds]
CTA:         [the single ask]
Effort:      [low / medium / high]
Why it works: [one sentence - the actual mechanism, not "it's engaging"]
```

Mix effort levels: at least five low-effort ideas the artist can shoot on a phone today.
A plan of ten high-effort concepts produces zero posts.

## 3. Captions

Write finished captions for the three strongest ideas - copy-paste ready, in the
artist's voice from the profile. Not descriptions of captions. Structure and length
rules are in `references/platform-playbooks.md`.

Each caption: hook line, one or two lines of substance, one CTA, then hashtags on a
separate line.

## 4. Titles and descriptions

For any idea heading to YouTube (Shorts or long-form), write the title and description
per `references/seo-metadata.md`. Titles under 60 characters, front-loaded with the
searchable term.

## 5. Hashtags

Per platform, per post. Layer them: 2-3 broad, 3-5 genre/niche, 1-2 community, 1 branded.
Never the same block on every post - repeated identical hashtag sets get downranked.
Avoid generic maximum-volume tags (#music, #fyp, #viral) as the primary strategy; they
put the artist in a feed with millions of competitors and no relevance signal.

## 6. Collaboration targets

Five creators who fit naturally: other artists, producers, dancers, editors, cosplayers,
podcasters - whoever matches the sound.

For each: who they are, roughly what size, why the audience overlaps, and the specific
collab format (duet, remix, feature, dance, sync to their edit). Prefer creators within
roughly 0.5x-3x the artist's own following - much larger targets do not reply, much
smaller ones do not move numbers.

If you cannot verify a creator currently exists and is active, describe the *type* and
where to search for them rather than inventing a handle. A fabricated @handle wastes the
artist's time and can point them at a stranger.

## 7. Outreach targets

Five targets across playlist curators, blogs, podcasts, DJs, and influencers. Follow the
sourcing and vetting rules in `references/outreach.md`, including the scam-curator
checks. Same rule as above: never invent a contact.

## 8. Outreach drafts

Personalized messages for the top three targets from section 7. Templates and the
personalization requirement are in `references/outreach.md`. Every message must contain
a line that could only have been written for that specific recipient.

## 9. Posting times

Give exact local times for today's posts, per platform. State the reasoning in one line.

Default starting points, to be replaced by the artist's own analytics as soon as they
exist - these are priors, not facts:

| Platform | Typical windows (local) |
|---|---|
| TikTok | 6-9am, 12-2pm, 7-11pm |
| Instagram Reels | 11am-1pm, 7-9pm |
| YouTube Shorts | 12-3pm, 5-8pm |
| Facebook Reels | 1-4pm |
| X / Threads | 8-10am, 5-7pm |
| Pinterest | 8-11pm |

Never schedule more than one post per platform in the same hour.

## 10. Yesterday's read

**Only if numbers were supplied.** If they were not, write exactly one line asking for
the specific export needed (see the data-honesty rule in `SKILL.md`) and move on. Do not
manufacture a review of posts you cannot see.

When data is present, use the diagnostic table in `references/metrics.md`: what
over-performed and the most likely reason, what under-performed and the most likely
reason, and one concrete change for today.

---

## Closing block

End every run with:

**Today's three actions** - the three things that matter most, in order. If the artist
does only these, the day counts.

**Log entry** - a row to append to the running log in `artist-profile.md`.
