# Metrics

What to track, what it means, and how to diagnose a bad week.

**Reminder:** you have no dashboard access. Every number in an analysis must come from
the artist. See the data-honesty rule in `SKILL.md`.

## The hierarchy

Not all metrics are equal. Ranked by how well they predict a real career:

**Tier 1 - intent.** These say someone chose the music.
- Saves / library adds
- Playlist adds (by real listeners, not curators)
- Repeat listeners
- Email signups
- Direct shares

**Tier 2 - engagement.** Real attention, not yet commitment.
- Watch time and average view duration
- Completion rate
- Comments (substantive ones)
- Follows gained per post
- Click-through to streaming

**Tier 3 - reach.** Necessary, but meaningless alone.
- Views, impressions, likes, follower count

**Optimize Tier 1. Diagnose with Tier 2. Never celebrate Tier 3 in isolation.** A video
with 500k views and 12 saves means the content entertained people who do not care about
the music - which is a content problem wearing a success costume.

## Benchmarks

Rough, for an artist under ~50k followers. Use the artist's own trailing average as the
real baseline; these are only for orientation when no history exists.

| Metric | Weak | Okay | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok completion rate | <20% | 25-40% | >50% |
| TikTok engagement rate | <3% | 4-8% | >10% |
| Reels engagement rate | <2% | 3-6% | >8% |
| Shorts avg view duration | <50% | 60-75% | >85% |
| YouTube CTR (long-form) | <2% | 4-6% | >8% |
| Spotify save rate | <5% | 8-15% | >20% |
| Email open rate | <20% | 25-35% | >40% |
| Email click rate | <2% | 3-5% | >8% |
| Social → stream CTR | <1% | 2-4% | >6% |

## Diagnosis

Match the symptom, not the vibe:

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Low views, good retention on those who watched | Hook is not stopping the scroll | Rewrite the first 2s. Keep everything else |
| High views, retention collapses at 3-5s | Hook overpromised | Deliver the payoff sooner; cut setup |
| High views, low saves and follows | Entertaining, but not converting to fandom | Put the music forward. Show the artist, not just the content |
| Good engagement, no streams | CTA missing, unclear, or link friction | One clear ask; check the link path works on mobile |
| Streams up, saves flat | Passive playlist listening | Ask directly for saves; give a reason to keep the song |
| Followers up, views flat | Follower base is not the content's audience | Check who followed; the content may be attracting non-listeners |
| Everything down at once | Posting gap, or a format that has exhausted itself | Check cadence first, then rotate pillar |
| One post spikes, nothing follows | No capture mechanism on the spike | Pin a comment with the link; follow up within 24h while attention lasts |

## Reading the data honestly

- **Small numbers are noisy.** Under a few thousand views, a 30% swing is usually
  nothing. Do not rewrite the strategy from one post
- **Give a post 7 days** before judging it. TikTok and Shorts distribution is lumpy, and
  posts recover
- **One variable at a time.** Changing hook, length, sound, and posting time together
  teaches nothing about which mattered
- **Correlation is not the mechanism.** A post going out at 7pm and doing well does not
  make 7pm the reason
- **Watch trailing averages, not single posts.** The 4-week trend is the signal; any
  individual post is mostly variance
- **Beware survivorship reasoning.** Copying what a viral artist did ignores the hundreds
  who did the same thing and were not seen
