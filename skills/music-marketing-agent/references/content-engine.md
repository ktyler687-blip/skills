# Content Engine

Hooks, pillars, and CTAs. Load when generating video ideas or captions.

## The first two seconds

Short-form retention is decided before the hook sentence finishes. Every concept must
specify what happens in frame one.

**Openers that work:**

- **Mid-motion** - start on a hand hitting a pad, a fader moving, a step. Motion in
  frame one stops the scroll.
- **Claim on screen** - "this sample is from a 1974 gospel record" before any talking.
- **Question with stakes** - "what does a $30 mic actually sound like next to a $3000 one"
- **Cold open on the hook** - drop straight into the loudest, catchiest 3 seconds of the
  song. No intro, no build.
- **Face, close, talking already mid-sentence** - implies the conversation started
  without them, which pulls people in.
- **Visible contradiction** - "everyone said this beat was too slow" over a fast cut.

**Openers that kill a video:** a logo, a fade from black, "hey guys," a slow pan across
an empty room, any text the viewer must read for 3 seconds before anything happens.

## Twelve pillars

Rotate these. An account that posts one pillar exclusively caps out fast.

| Pillar | What it does | Formats that work |
|---|---|---|
| **Behind the scenes** | Builds parasocial investment; highest-converting pillar for turning viewers into fans | Beat from scratch, take that did not make it, vocal comping, gear, the mistake |
| **Song story** | Gives the song meaning, which is what makes people save it | What happened, who it is about, the line that was almost cut, the voice memo it started as |
| **Performance** | Proves the artist is real and good | Live take, one-take vocal, stripped version, unusual location |
| **Lifestyle** | Context and personality between drops | Day in the life, tour van, the job before music, what is on repeat |
| **Fan questions** | Free content, and it makes people feel seen | Reply-to-comment videos, Q&A stickers, "you asked, here it is" |
| **Trend participation** | Reach into non-followers | Trending sound + genuine angle. Skip anything that requires being someone else |
| **Duets / stitches** | Borrows another audience | Duet a fan's cover, stitch a music take, react to a producer's beat |
| **Reactions** | Cheap and algorithm-friendly | First listen, reacting to fan edits, hearing own song in public |
| **Lyric videos** | Search demand exists; also drives comment-a-lyric | Kinetic type, handwritten, on-location text |
| **Visualizers** | Fills the release calendar cheaply | Loop, Canvas, abstract, cover art motion |
| **Cinematic clips** | The prestige asset; use sparingly | One strong visual concept, treated properly |
| **Live snippets** | Social proof | Crowd singing back, soundcheck, small-room energy |

**Ratio that works:** roughly 40% behind-the-scenes and story, 25% performance, 20%
trend and collaborative, 15% lifestyle. Adjust to what the weekly report proves.

## Caption structure

```
[Hook line - can stand alone, must earn the "more" tap]
[1-2 lines of substance - the story, the detail, the joke]
[One CTA]
[Hashtags on their own line]
```

Front-load. Most platforms truncate at 100-125 characters, and most viewers never expand.

**Engagement prompts that get real answers** - specific, low-effort, no wrong response:

- "comment the lyric that got you"
- "which version - the one at 0:14 or 0:31?"
- "what should this one be called"
- "who does this remind you of"

Avoid "thoughts?" and "let me know what you think." Too open to answer quickly.

## Calls to action

One per post. Match the CTA to the funnel stage - asking a first-time viewer to join a
mailing list is a wasted ask.

| Stage | Situation | CTA |
|---|---|---|
| Cold | First contact, non-follower | "follow for more" / comment prompt |
| Warm | Knows the artist, has not streamed | "listen now, link in bio" |
| Warm | Has streamed once | **"save it"** / "add it to your playlist" |
| Hot | Repeat listener | "share it with one person" |
| Hot | Engaged fan | "join the list" / presave |

**Save is the most valuable ask** on a release. It signals intent to Spotify's
recommendation systems in a way a single passive stream does not, and it puts the song
in a library the listener actually returns to.

## Sixteen reusable formats

Concepts that can be refilled with any song, indefinitely:

1. "I made a beat out of [unexpected source]"
2. "This song almost didn't come out - here's why"
3. Producer breakdown - strip the track to stems, layer by layer
4. "Rate this unreleased snippet 1-10"
5. Same hook, three genres
6. "The lyric everyone gets wrong"
7. Studio reaction to hearing the master for the first time
8. "POV: [scene the song soundtracks]"
9. Sped-up vs original A/B
10. Answering the meanest comment
11. "Songs that sound like [popular artist] but aren't"
12. Making a song from a fan's one-word prompt
13. "This took 6 months, here's the 15 seconds that mattered"
14. Playing the song for someone whose reaction is unfiltered
15. Live one-take in an unusual room
16. "Where I was when I wrote this" - shot on location

## Hashtags

Layer, do not spray:

- 2-3 broad genre (#hiphop, #rnb)
- 3-5 niche/subgenre - this is where discovery actually happens
- 1-2 community (#unsignedartist, #producerlife)
- 1 branded, consistent across every post

Rotate sets between posts. Identical hashtag blocks post after post read as automation
and get suppressed. 3-5 tags is plenty on TikTok; Instagram tolerates more but relevance
beats volume on both.
