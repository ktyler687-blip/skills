# Platform Playbooks

Per-platform mechanics and the repurposing matrix. Specs change - verify anything
critical before a paid or high-stakes push.

## One shoot, many cuts

Plan the repurpose before filming. Shoot vertical 9:16, keep the center third clear of
action, and leave headroom so a 1:1 or 4:5 crop still works.

| Source asset | Becomes |
|---|---|
| One 60s studio session | TikTok (25s), Reel (20s, different hook), Short (35s), Pinterest Idea Pin, X native clip, 3 stills, newsletter GIF |
| One live performance | Short vertical cut, full-length YouTube, audio-only for Threads/X, still for Instagram, Facebook Reel |
| One lyric video | Shorts loop, Instagram carousel of lyric cards, Pinterest pins per line, Spotify Canvas |

**Always re-cut the hook per platform.** Do not cross-post the same file five times. Beyond
the visible watermark penalty, the same opening frame that worked on TikTok often fails on
Shorts, where the audience arrives from search rather than a feed.

**Never post a file with another platform's watermark.** Both TikTok and Instagram
downrank competitor watermarks. Export clean from the editor.

## TikTok

- 9:16, 21-34s is the reliable band for music content. Under 15s limits watch-time
  accumulation; over 60s needs a real reason
- Keep captions and stickers out of the right-side action rail and the bottom third
- Use the artist's own track as the sound wherever possible - it builds the sound page,
  which is what lets other creators find and use it
- Post 1-3x daily if capacity allows, at least 3-4x weekly
- Reply to comments with video replies - it is a free second post with the first post's
  audience already attached
- Watch **completion rate and rewatches** above all else
- Do not delete an underperformer in the first 48h; TikTok distribution is slow and lumpy

## Instagram

- Reels are the discovery surface; feed and Stories serve existing followers
- 15-30s Reels, 9:16, with a cover frame chosen deliberately for the grid
- Original audio builds a reusable audio page - same logic as TikTok
- Carousels work well for lyrics, story-behind-the-song, and photo dumps, and get a
  second impression when someone does not swipe through the first time
- Stories: link stickers, polls, question boxes. Question boxes feed the fan-questions
  pillar directly
- Collabs (co-authored posts) put the content in both audiences' feeds - the strongest
  free reach mechanism on the platform
- **Shares and saves** are the metrics that matter

## YouTube

Two different products, do not conflate them.

**Shorts** - discovery. Under 60s, vertical, hook-led, high volume.

**Long-form** - depth and search. Music videos, full performances, production
breakdowns, vlogs.

- Titles under 60 characters, searchable term first
- Thumbnails: face or one clear focal object, under 4 words of text, readable at
  thumbnail size
- Description: strong first two lines (that is what shows), then links, then timestamps,
  then credits
- Pinned comment with the streaming link
- Playlists for every release - it chains autoplay and lifts session time
- Watch **CTR and average view duration** together; one without the other misleads

## Facebook

- Reels, cross-posted from Instagram but re-captioned - the audience skews older and
  responds to more context
- Longer captions perform better than on Instagram
- Groups are underrated for genre communities, but participate for weeks before posting
  a link; drive-by promo gets removed and often banned

## X

- Native video only. Link-out posts get suppressed
- Short, dry, punchy. Threads work for song stories
- Quote-tweet fan reactions
- Post 3-5x daily if maintained at all; the timeline moves too fast for less

## Threads

- Conversational, text-first, low-polish. Behind-the-scenes musings, half-thoughts,
  questions
- Replies drive reach more than posts do - be in other people's threads
- Do not just mirror X output

## Pinterest

- Long-tail search, not a social feed. A pin can still drive traffic a year later
- Vertical 2:3, keyword-rich titles and descriptions
- Lyric graphics, aesthetic boards tied to the song's mood, "songs for [activity]" boards
- Every pin links to the smart link or site
- The slowest channel to show results and one of the most durable

## Snapchat Spotlight

- Vertical, fast, native-feeling. Polished ads-style content underperforms badly
- Use the artist's sound from the Snapchat music library if available
- Low effort to repurpose; treat as a bonus surface, not a priority

## Streaming platforms

**Spotify** - Claim Spotify for Artists. Pitch every release at least 7 days out.
Maintain Artist Pick, Canvas on every track, a written bio with keywords, and current
photos. Artist playlists are underused - a good one earns follows.

**Apple Music** - Claim Apple Music for Artists. Lyrics are a real discovery surface
here; submit them.

**YouTube Music** - Flows from the YouTube channel. Verify the Official Artist Channel.

**Amazon Music** - Claim the profile; less traffic, near-zero competition for it.

**Audiomack** - Genuinely valuable for hip-hop, R&B, Afrobeats. Free uploads, an
engaged discovery audience, and curation reachable without gatekeepers.

## Posting frequency

Sustainable beats maximal. Pick what the profile's capacity field can actually support:

| Platform | Minimum | Good |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 3x/week | 1-2x/day |
| Instagram Reels | 3x/week | 1x/day |
| YouTube Shorts | 3x/week | 1x/day |
| Facebook Reels | 2x/week | 4x/week |
| X | 3x/week | 3-5x/day |
| Threads | 3x/week | 1-2x/day |
| Pinterest | 3x/week | 1x/day |
| Email | 2x/month | weekly |
